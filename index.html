Bio

I am a second-year PhD student in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at University of California, Los Angeles, advised by Prof. Xiaowu Dai.

My research focuses on uncertainty quantification of LLM rankings based on human preferences. In particular, I am working on constructing prompt-dependent confidence intervals for the rankings of LLMs. Building on this framework, I am currently designing a routing system for LLMs under cost constraints.

Prior to my PhD, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from the California Institute of Technology.
